# WizardCard 味契系列卡图设计规范

## Asset brief

Create 17 independent, transparent-background card illustration sprites for the 味契 series. The sprites are used inside WizardCard 2.0 cards and must match the existing high-resolution modern pixel-art illustration language.

## Shared visual direction

- 1024×1024 RGBA PNG, with generous transparent margin on every side.
- Crisp chunky pixel clusters, stepped silhouettes, dark warm outlines, and limited shaded ramps.
- Antique gold and teal are the shared magical-material colors; each card adds a subject-specific accent color.
- Use luminous lines, glyphs, circles, and angular marks to communicate spellwork, runes, and formations.
- One readable central subject per sprite; holes inside rings and formations remain transparent.
- No card frame, scenery, floor, written text, digits, watermark, UI, checkerboard, or photo-real rendering.
